# Tracker brand assets

Brand marks identify outbound links only. They do not imply affiliation or endorsement. The Geld source license remains AGPL-3.0-only. The original asset license files remain in the upstream packages. No Geld license file is added or changed. Trademark rights remain with each provider. Required distribution notices have been prepared privately for the owner to approve before publishing.

Assets were copied without changing their artwork (the Atlassian SVG strings gained the XML namespace needed for standalone images) on November 1, 2026:

- Jira and Bitbucket: official Atlassian `@atlaskit/logo` 23.3.1 brand icons, Apache 2.0.
- Monday: official `@vibe/icons` 4.2.0 `MondayLogoOutline`, MIT.
- GitLab: official `@gitlab/svgs` 3.171.0 `gitlab_logo`, MIT.
- GitHub, Linear and YouTrack: the provider brand assets distributed with official GitLab integration icons in `@gitlab/svgs` 3.171.0. These are brand graphics, not generic replacement glyphs.
- Asana: brand path from Simple Icons 16.36.0, whose source and guidelines both point to [Asana's brand page](https://asana.com/brand), CC0 icon distribution. This is the one asset distributed by a third-party icon catalog rather than a provider-owned package.

[Linear brand guidance](https://linear.app/brand) requires unmodified graphics and prohibits implying endorsement. Marks are sized with CSS, retain their original artwork, and are not combined into the Geld logo. The owner should include tracker branding in the pre-launch legal review, particularly the third-party-distributed Asana asset. No brand marks are used in marketing claims.
